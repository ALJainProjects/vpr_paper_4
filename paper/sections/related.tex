\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Place recognition in the body.}
Off-the-shelf models are already used in the body: beside the
landmark-trained baseline of~\cite{morlana2021selfsupervised}, NetVLAD retrieves candidate views in
ureteroscopy~\cite{li2026automated}, off-the-shelf local matchers (LoFTR
with outdoor weights, LightGlue) run inside ColonMapper and
ColonSLAM~\cite{morlana2024colonmapper,morlana2024topological}, and a
training-free foundation-model method re-identifies regions after scope
reinsertion~\cite{soberanismukul2026global}. Apart from that baseline,
ColonSLAM's off-the-shelf LightGlue baseline and Colon10k's unread
evaluation, we found no measurement of an off-the-shelf model's
place-recognition accuracy in the body, and no oracle ceiling
(for~\cite{soberanismukul2026global}, as far as its abstract shows).

\paragraph{Zero-shot VPR and frozen foundation features.}
Moving a frozen VPR zoo to a texture-poor domain is an established
genre~\cite{keetha2024anyloc,larsen2026longterm}, none of it medical; we
borrow its protocol and add the domain and the ceiling. Frozen general and
endoscopy foundation models have been compared for object- and class-level
retrieval in GI endoscopy~\cite{parolari2026contrastive,kim2025improving},
frozen DINOv2 being a strong baseline~\cite{parolari2026contrastive}, but as
far as we found never for same-place retrieval; endoscopy foundation
models~\cite{wang2023foundation,batic2024endovit,hirsch2023selfsupervised}
are usually evaluated after fine-tuning.

\paragraph{Ceilings, ambiguity and feature reliance.}
The oracle ceiling---the fraction of queries that at least one method
localizes---was introduced for VPR, for pairs of techniques, as an estimate
of the maximum achievable performance~\cite{waheed2021improving}; we apply it
to a zoo. TOPO-Bench already measures dataset ambiguity across indoor and
outdoor environments and argues that it is largely
method-independent~\cite{wang2025topobench}; we propose no such measure.
The texture-bias account of CNNs is contested: under controlled cue
suppression they rely mostly on local shape, and medical-imaging models
emphasize colour~\cite{burgert2025imagenettrained}; VPR descriptors can
also retrieve by condition rather than place~\cite{kim2026visual}.
