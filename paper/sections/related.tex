\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Place recognition in the body.}
Colon10k's authors report ``an extensive evaluation on different existing
place recognition techniques'' on its endoscopist-verified same-place
intervals and state that ``traditional image retrieval techniques do not
generalize well in colonic images''~\cite{ma2021colon10k}. That evaluation,
on the same dataset, is the closest to ours; we read only its abstract, so we
cannot say which techniques it ran or whether any ran off the shelf, and we
compare with none of its numbers. Morlana et al.'s
off-the-shelf landmark-trained baseline on real colonoscopy lost recall as
near-duplicate frames were excluded~\cite{morlana2021selfsupervised}, and a
topological mapping system relocalizes with retrieval fine-tuned on
colonoscopy~\cite{morlana2024colonmapper}. On robotic capsules and robotic
endoscopes we found no retrieval of same-place frames with a learned global
descriptor (Sec.~\ref{sec:intro}).

\paragraph{Zero-shot VPR and oracle ceilings.}
Moving a frozen VPR zoo to a texture-poor domain is an established
genre~\cite{keetha2024anyloc,larsen2026longterm}, none of it medical. The
oracle ceiling, the fraction of queries that at least one method localizes,
was introduced for pairs of VPR techniques~\cite{waheed2021improving}; we
apply it to a zoo. Comparisons across VPR datasets confound domain with
ground-truth kind and set-up~\cite{schubert2021makes}.
