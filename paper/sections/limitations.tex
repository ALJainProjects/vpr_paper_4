\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Not robot data.} Colon10k is conventional, human-driven
colonoscope video from one centre. Capsule optics, illumination and motion
differ, and capsule frame rates are low and may be
adaptive~\cite{cao2024robotic,bause2026capsulemotion}, so a window counted in
colonoscope frames has no fixed meaning in time or distance on a capsule. No
controller was run, so Recall@1 is not the localization accuracy a closed
loop would see. The models would run off the robot: capsule compute is
microcontroller-class~\cite{bause2026capsulemotion}, and we measure no
latency, memory or power. We ran no comparison with magnetic localization.

\paragraph{Offline, within one procedure.} A query's database is its own
case, later frames included, so a query may be answered by a revisit that an
on-line relocalizer would not yet have seen; we did not count such queries.
All revisits lie inside one short procedure (databases of \res{pc-ndb-c08}
to \res{pc-ndb-c14} frames per test case); relocalization across procedures,
as at follow-up, is not tested.

\paragraph{Small, single-source evidence.} The gate rests on \res{gate-nq}
answerable queries in \res{gate-ncases} cases of one dataset, a low power the protocol stated before any result; the intervals
are wide, and on the \res{val-ncases} validation cases \res{val-nnontriv} of
\res{val-nzoo} members were non-trivial, so the test outcome may not
replicate. The ground truth is of
one kind, endoscopist intervals not known to be exhaustive, so an unlabelled
revisit lowers recall and inflates the zoo-unsolved fraction. The \res{gate-ndrop} unanswerable
queries are dropped, so the results cover only queries with a same-place
frame outside the window.

\paragraph{Zoo, statistics, work not run.} Raw DINOv2 was to join the zoo
only if its wrapper existed at Stage~B, and it did not, so the pre-registered
contrast of VPR training with raw DINOv2 (H-E1b) is untestable; no medical-domain encoder is included (no weights were obtained);
AnyLoc enters only as GeM. The bounds carry no multiplicity correction.
The pre-registered secondary run with field-of-view
masking was not run (masking was never wired into the evaluation code). The ceiling on phantoms with geometric ground truth
\pending{E2 on C3VD/C3VDv2: not run}, the phantom feature-reliance
contrasts \pending{E4: not run}, an anatomical-section probe
\pending{E3: not run} and sequence priors \pending{E5: not run} are not
part of this study.
