\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Weak data; no clinical claim.} Nothing here evaluates navigation,
workflow or patient outcome. Phantoms are rigid silicone with pose from
robot kinematics and 2D--3D
registration~\cite{bobrow2023colonoscopy,golhar2026c3vdv2}, and results on
them make no claim about patients. Real-patient evidence is Colon10k (twenty
short sequences, one centre~\cite{ma2021colon10k}), which carries no pose,
plus, if obtainable, EndoMapper covisibility, a structure-from-motion pseudo
ground truth~\cite{azagra2023endomapper} available by request (no
data-access agreement is signed).

\paragraph{``Zoo-unsolved'' is conditional.} The ceiling is relative to the
zoo, ground truth, window and $k$, and bounds only per-query selection
(Sec.~\ref{sec:ceiling}). Unlabelled revisits in Colon10k would count correct
retrievals as errors and inflate $I$, hence the audit; excluded adaptation
may solve zoo-unsolved queries; and on real data, deformation, specularities,
blur and debris~\cite{schmidt2024tracking} are confounded with view
ambiguity, which strata reduce and only phantom contrasts isolate.

\paragraph{Unread prior work and unbuilt parts.} Colon10k's results
table~\cite{ma2021colon10k} is unread (which techniques it evaluated is
unknown to us), the model of~\cite{soberanismukul2026global} is
unidentified, and ColonMapper's protocol labels are unverified. The overlap
ground truth, the raw-DINOv2, medical-arm and further AnyLoc wrappers, and
the preprocessing wiring must be built before the runs that use them.
