\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Weak data; no clinical claim.} Nothing here evaluates navigation,
workflow or patient outcome. Phantom results are in-silico: rigid silicone,
pose from robot kinematics and 2D--3D registration~\cite{bobrow2023colonoscopy,golhar2026c3vdv2}. Real-patient
retrieval evidence is Colon10k (twenty short sequences, one
centre~\cite{ma2021colon10k}) plus, if obtainable, EndoMapper covisibility,
which is structure-from-motion pseudo ground
truth~\cite{azagra2023endomapper}. No public dataset pairs real-patient
images with frame-level pose~\cite{cold2025mapping}. EndoMapper's region
labels cover five sequences, so E3 rests on REAL-Colon; clustered intervals
will be wide. EndoMapper is available by request, and no data-access
agreement has been signed yet.

\paragraph{``Irreducible'' is conditional.} The ceiling is relative to the
zoo, the ground truth and the window, and the irreducible fraction depends
on the embedding~\cite{renggli2021evaluating}. Distance thresholds overestimate
recall~\cite{larsen2026longterm}, hence two geometric definitions. Excluded
adaptation~\cite{zaffar2025overlooked} may recover ``irreducible'' queries.
On real data, deformation~\cite{lamarca2021defslam}, specularities, blur and
debris~\cite{schmidt2024tracking} are confounded with view ambiguity; quality
strata reduce this, and only phantom contrasts isolate texture from
geometry.

\paragraph{Comparisons and unread prior work.} The transfer gap and the
streets-versus-colon ceilings confound domain with
set-up~\cite{schubert2021makes}. We could not
read Colon10k's results table, so which techniques it evaluated, and whether
off-the-shelf, is unknown to us~\cite{ma2021colon10k}; the model
of~\cite{soberanismukul2026global} is unidentified. The ColonMapper protocol
labels and the GastroNet-5M and EndoDINO weights are unverified.
