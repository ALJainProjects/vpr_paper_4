\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Not robot data.} Colon10k is conventional, human-driven
colonoscope video from one centre. Capsule optics, illumination and motion
differ, and capsule frame rates are low and may be
adaptive~\cite{cao2024robotic,bause2026capsulemotion}, so a window counted in
colonoscope frames has no fixed meaning in time or distance on a capsule. No
controller was run, so Recall@1 is not the localization accuracy a closed
loop would see. The models would run off the robot: capsule compute is
microcontroller-class~\cite{bause2026capsulemotion}, and we measure no
latency, memory or power. We ran no comparison with magnetic localization.

\paragraph{Small, single-source evidence.} The gate rests on \res{gate-nq}
answerable queries in \res{gate-ncases} cases of one dataset from one
centre, a low power the protocol stated before any result; the intervals
are wide, and on the \res{val-ncases} validation cases no member was
non-trivial, so the test outcome may not replicate. The ground truth is of
one kind, endoscopist intervals that are not known to be exhaustive; an
unlabelled revisit turns a correct retrieval into an error, lowering recall
and inflating the zoo-unsolved fraction. The \res{gate-ndrop} unanswerable
queries are dropped, so the results cover only queries with a same-place
frame outside the window, and that set changes with the window.

\paragraph{Zoo and statistics.} Raw DINOv2 is absent, so VPR training is not
separated from its backbone; no medical-domain encoder is included (no
weights were obtained); AnyLoc enters only as GeM. The split condition of the
gate was disabled, the bounds carry no multiplicity correction, and the zoo
ceiling bounds only per-query selection among these members.

\pending{E2 on C3VD/C3VDv2: not run}, the phantom feature-reliance
contrasts \pending{E4: not run}, an anatomical-section probe
\pending{E3: not run} and sequence priors \pending{E5: not run} are not
part of this study.
