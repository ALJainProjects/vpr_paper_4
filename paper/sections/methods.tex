\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Frozen models}
\label{sec:zoo}
The \emph{outdoor VPR zoo} $\Zout$ holds NetVLAD~\cite{arandjelovic2016netvlad},
CosPlace~\cite{berton2022rethinking}, EigenPlaces~\cite{berton2023eigenplaces}
and MixVPR~\cite{alibey2023mixvpr} (CNN-based), SALAD~\cite{izquierdo2024optimal}
and MegaLoc~\cite{berton2025megaloc} (DINOv2-based), and
BoQ~\cite{alibey2024boq}, whose CNN and DINOv2 variants join their families.
The \emph{generic arm} $\Zgen$ holds raw DINOv2~\cite{oquab2023dinov2} (CLS
token; GeM-pooled patch tokens), which separates VPR training from the
backbone, and AnyLoc~\cite{keetha2024anyloc} with GeM aggregation, which needs
no vocabulary. AnyLoc-VLAD builds its vocabulary from the target database, so it is
reported as a separate \emph{target-adapted} condition, outside every
zero-shot claim. The
\emph{medical arm} $\Zmed$, also frozen, holds EndoViT~\cite{batic2024endovit}
(surgical pretraining), Endo-FM~\cite{wang2023foundation} (video) and the
colonoscopy encoders of~\cite{hirsch2023selfsupervised}. GastroNet-5M~\cite{jong2026gastronet5m}
and EndoDINO~\cite{dermyer2025endodino} (to $\Zmed$) and the landmark-trained
anchor of~\cite{morlana2021selfsupervised} (to $\Zout$) join only if their
weights prove obtainable.
\pending{pre-registered: checkpoint, input size and descriptor dimension per
model; Endo-FM frame-embedding recipe} No parameter is updated;
reference-set fine-tuning~\cite{zaffar2025overlooked}, adapters and
re-ranking are excluded by design, and some ``recoverable'' error may be
recoverable by them. Descriptors are L2-normalized and ranked by exhaustive
cosine similarity.

\subsection{Datasets and ground truth}
\label{sec:data}
\paragraph{Colon10k (real).} Twenty short sequences from a single centre,
undistorted to low-resolution pinhole frames~\cite{ma2021colon10k}. Per
case, a matchings file lists for each annotated query the intervals of
frames that an endoscopist verified as the same place; the loader expands
them into positive sets. Published image totals disagree between sources, so we
count what we acquire: \pending{Colon10k frame, query and positive counts as
acquired}. Each query searches the other frames of its sequence. Frames within an exclusion window $W$ of the query leave
both database and positives, as near-duplicates inflate
recall~\cite{morlana2021selfsupervised}; queries left without a positive are
dropped and counted. We could not read Colon10k's own protocol and do
not compare with its numbers.

\paragraph{C3VD and C3VDv2 (phantom).} Silicone phantoms with per-frame pose
and depth~\cite{bobrow2023colonoscopy}; C3VDv2 adds two geometries with four
textures each and, for every debris-laden video, a clean twin on the
identical trajectory~\cite{golhar2026c3vdv2}. Queries come from one
recording and the database from the other recordings of the same phantom,
whose poses share the phantom frame through the dataset's 2D--3D
registration (the loader checks this). The primary ground truth is
\emph{view overlap}: the fraction of a query's valid pixels whose
depth-back-projected points land in the database view with consistent depth,
positive at $o^\star$ or above. The secondary one is \emph{pose and angle}:
camera centres within $\tau$ and optical axes within $\theta$. Pose radius
alone is never used, since nearby cameras in a tube may face different ways
and share no view~\cite{larsen2026longterm,wei2025breaking}. Every phantom
result is in-silico.

\paragraph{Section labels (real, cross-patient).} REAL-Colon's frame-level
section labels, from four centres~\cite{biffi2024realcolon,biffi2025temporal},
are the E3 set; EndoMapper's region labels, on only five
sequences~\cite{azagra2023endomapper}, give a leave-one-sequence-out repeat.
No public dataset pairs real-patient endoscopic images with frame-level
pose~\cite{cold2025mapping}.

\paragraph{Preprocessing.} A field-of-view mask and a specular-highlight
mask~\cite{schmidt2024tracking} precede resizing to each model's native
input; their parameters are fixed in advance, and the unmodified street
pipeline runs as an ablation.

\subsection{Oracle ceiling and error decomposition}
\label{sec:ceiling}
Let $c_m(q)\in\{0,1\}$ mark that model $m$'s top-1 retrieval for query $q$
is a positive under a given ground truth and window. For a zoo $Z$ the
oracle ceiling~\cite{waheed2021improving} is
$C(Z)=\frac{1}{|Q|}\sum_q \max_{m\in Z} c_m(q)$ and the irreducible fraction
is $I(Z)=1-C(Z)$. Each model's top-1 error splits exactly as
$1-R_m=\rho_m(Z)+I(Z)$, where
$\rho_m(Z)=\frac{1}{|Q|}\sum_q \bigl(1-c_m(q)\bigr)\max_{m'\in Z} c_{m'}(q)$
is the recoverable part: queries $m$ misses but another member retrieves,
after the split of~\cite{vasconcelos2004minimum}. The ceiling is
zoo-relative by construction, so we report (a) $C$ and $I$ for $\Zout$, $\Zgen$, $\Zmed$
and their union $\Zall$, since the irreducible fraction depends on the
embedding~\cite{renggli2021evaluating}; (b) the zoo-size curve (mean of
$C(S)$ over subsets $S\subseteq Z$ of each size) and each leave-one-out
contribution $C(Z)-C(Z\setminus\{m\})$; and (c) the \emph{chance ceiling} of
$|Z|$ independent random rankings,
$\Crand(|Z|)=\frac{1}{|Q|}\sum_q \bigl[1-(1-P_q/N_q)^{|Z|}\bigr]$ for $P_q$
positives among $N_q$ database frames, because a union over many models
collects chance hits and Colon10k's positive intervals can be wide. A
nearest-neighbour Bayes-error bracket~\cite{cover1967nearest} is at most a
labelled heuristic here: with a handful of positives per query in
high-dimensional spaces it is far outside its reliable sample
regime~\cite{wheat2025bayes}. Nothing in this paper is a Bayes-error
estimate.

\subsection{Baselines and statistics}
\label{sec:stats}
Two training-free baselines anchor ``non-trivial'': a random ranking, whose
expected Recall@$k$ per query is $1-\binom{N_q-P_q}{k}\big/\binom{N_q}{k}$,
and handcrafted global descriptors (a downsampled raw-pixel thumbnail and one
fixed handcrafted descriptor). Confidence intervals are cluster bootstraps
over sequences (Colon10k), recordings (C3VD) or patients and centres
(REAL-Colon); model contrasts are paired within resamples, with Holm
correction per pre-registered family. No number will be typed by hand: each
comes from a hash-verified snapshot of the harness results.
