\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Data and ground truth}
\label{sec:data}
Colon10k~\cite{ma2021colon10k} holds twenty short colonoscopy sequences
(cases) from one centre, undistorted to low-resolution pinhole frames. For
each case, a matchings file lists, for each annotated query, the frame
intervals that an endoscopist verified as the same place. The undocumented
file format was read from the files before any model was run
(Sec.~\ref{sec:protocol}). The positives of a query are the frames of its own
case inside its intervals. Frames within $W$ frames of the query in the
case's frame list, the query included, are removed from both the database and
the positives, since near-duplicate neighbours inflate
recall~\cite{morlana2021selfsupervised}; a query left without a positive is
unanswerable and is dropped and counted. The database is the query's own
case, frames recorded after the query included, so the test is offline
(Sec.~\ref{sec:limitations}).
Correctness at $k$ is computed over the full ranking after the removed frames
are taken out, and ties count against the model: a negative tied with the
best positive ranks first.

Before any data were seen, a seeded permutation of the case identifiers set
aside validation cases for method development; the other
\res{gate-ncases} cases form the test split, on which the gate was scored
once. One validation case proved unusable (its intervals reference frames
beyond the released images) and was dropped before any model was run. At the
gate window the test split has \res{cnt-test10-total} annotated queries, of
which \res{gate-ndrop} are unanswerable, leaving \res{gate-nq}.

\subsection{Frozen models and baselines}
\label{sec:zoo}
The gate zoo $\Zgate$ holds \res{gate-nzoo} frozen models with their released
weights. The VPR models, trained on street imagery (MegaLoc on a union of
VPR and landmark datasets), are CNN-based NetVLAD on
VGG16~\cite{arandjelovic2016netvlad}, CosPlace on ResNet-18, ResNet-50 and
ResNet-101~\cite{berton2022rethinking}, EigenPlaces~\cite{berton2023eigenplaces},
MixVPR~\cite{alibey2023mixvpr} and BoQ~\cite{alibey2024boq} on ResNet-50, and
DINOv2-based~\cite{oquab2023dinov2} SALAD~\cite{izquierdo2024optimal},
MegaLoc~\cite{berton2025megaloc}, BoQ on DINOv2 and
CricaVPR~\cite{lu2024cricavpr}. AnyLoc~\cite{keetha2024anyloc} enters in its
training-free GeM form on DINOv2 ViT-G/14 patch features, following the
authors' code. No parameter is updated and no endoscopy-specific
preprocessing (masking) is applied. Every model and the thumbnail receive
the harness's generic input: the frame upsampled with its aspect ratio kept,
padded with black to a 448-pixel square and ImageNet-normalized (MixVPR
resizes it to its fixed input size). Descriptors are L2-normalized and
ranked by exhaustive inner product.
Raw DINOv2 and medical-domain encoders are not in the zoo
(Sec.~\ref{sec:limitations}).

Chance is the exact probability that a random ranking puts a positive
first, $p_q=P_q/N_q$ for $P_q$ positives among $N_q$ candidates, with mean
$\Rrand$; random rankings never enter the zoo. The one handcrafted comparator
fixed in advance is a raw-pixel thumbnail: a colour tiny image, area-averaged,
zero-mean and L2-normalized, so that the inner product is a normalized
cross-correlation, with its default settings (Recall@1 $\Rhand$). A colour
histogram and one draw of an empirical random ranking are reported for
information only.

\subsection{The gate and its statistics}
\label{sec:gate}
The gate is evaluated at $k=\res{gate-k}$ and $W=\res{gate-window}$ frames on
the test split. A member $m$ \emph{beats chance} if $\LB(R_m-\Rrand)$
exceeds \res{gate-minmargin-chance-pts} percentage points and is
\emph{non-trivial} if, in addition, $\LB(R_m-\Rhand)>\res{gate-minmargin-comp}$; both margins
($\Drand$, $\Dhand$) are paired per query. The outcome is \texttt{GO} if at
least \res{gate-minpass} members are non-trivial and the error split is
meaningful, \texttt{NO\_GO} if no member beats chance, and
\texttt{OWNER\_REVIEW} otherwise, a case for which the protocol at first
defined no action (Sec.~\ref{sec:protocol}). A meaningful split needs lower
bounds on the zoo-unsolved fraction and on the best member's
selection-recoverable fraction (Sec.~\ref{sec:ceiling}) above two thresholds,
both set to $-\infty$ before any data were acquired (the split is this
study's measurement either way), so \texttt{GO} rests on the count of
non-trivial members alone. $\LB$ and $\UB$ are the ends of a central
\res{gate-cilevel} percentile bootstrap interval over whole cases
(\res{gate-nboot} replicates, each the pooled mean over the queries of the
resampled cases). The same level is used for every bound, with no correction
for multiplicity. The best member $m^\star$ is the one of highest Recall@1 on
the full test split, chosen once. The window and the database scope are
derived from the ground truth and checked against the protocol, not declared
by the caller.

\subsection{Zoo ceiling and error split}
\label{sec:ceiling}
Let $c_m(q)\in\{0,1\}$ mark that member $m$ ranks a positive first for query
$q$, and $R_m$ its mean (Recall@1). With $o(q)=\max_{m\in\Zgate}c_m(q)$, the
\emph{zoo ceiling} (after~\cite{waheed2021improving}) is
$C=\frac{1}{|Q|}\sum_q o(q)$ and the \emph{zoo-unsolved} fraction is $I=1-C$.
Each member's error splits exactly as $1-R_m=\rho_m+I$, where
$\rho_m=\frac{1}{|Q|}\sum_q\bigl(1-c_m(q)\bigr)\,o(q)$ is the share of
\emph{all} queries that $m$ misses and another member solves: its
\emph{selection-recoverable} error. Hence $\rbest=C-R_{m^\star}$ is also the
headroom of an oracle per-query selector over the best member. On this query set, ground truth and window, $C$ upper-bounds
the Recall@1 of any selector that returns one member's answer per query. It
bounds no model outside the zoo, nor score fusion, re-ranking or adaptation,
which can return an item no member ranks first; chance hits inflate it, and
ground-truth errors can move it either way. $I$ is an oracle miss rate, not a
Bayes error, and ``zoo-unsolved'' always means \emph{for this zoo, this ground
truth and this exclusion window}. The chance ceiling
$\Crand=\frac{1}{|Q|}\sum_q\bigl[1-(1-p_q)^{|\Zgate|}\bigr]$ is the expected
ceiling of as many independent random rankings as the zoo has members.
