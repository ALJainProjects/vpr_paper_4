\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Colonoscopy reconstructions break into disconnected
fragments~\cite{ma2021rnnslam}, and linking them or detecting a revisit is a
place-recognition problem. Bag-of-words place recognition fails in the
colon~\cite{elvira2024cudasiftslam}, while retrieval networks fine-tuned on
colonoscopy now drive topological mapping and relocalization in real
procedures~\cite{morlana2024colonmapper,morlana2024topological}. Endoscopic
localization as image retrieval is thus established, from retargeting across
examinations~\cite{ye2017image} to an endoscopist-verified
benchmark~\cite{ma2021colon10k} and full-procedure
data~\cite{azagra2023endomapper}. We claim no part of that formulation.

We ask a narrower question: when retrieval fails in the colon, \emph{whose}
failure is it? Off-street, outdoor VPR models lose recall, DINOv2-based ones
least~\cite{keetha2024anyloc,larsen2026longterm}, and the colon adds low
texture, specularities, deformation and repetitive
folds~\cite{schmidt2024tracking,lamarca2021defslam}. An error may be a
failure of the \emph{representation}, which a better model could recover, or
genuine \emph{view ambiguity}: translation ambiguity along the tube already
limits registration on phantoms~\cite{jiang2025c3vdreg}, and mapping
pipelines discard frames humans judge unrecognizable~\cite{morlana2024colonmapper}.
The remedies differ, and no study we found separates the two for retrieval.

Zero-shot evidence exists, and we build on it rather than claim it. Morlana
et al.~\cite{morlana2021selfsupervised} ran a landmark- and city-trained
retrieval CNN off-the-shelf on a held-out real colonoscopy: it trailed its
colonoscopy-fine-tuned counterpart by only single-digit Recall@1 margins, and
its recall fell steeply as near-duplicate frames were excluded. Colon10k
itself evaluated existing place-recognition techniques~\cite{ma2021colon10k}
(which ones, we could not determine). To our knowledge (search to September
2026), no prior work systematically benchmarks the 2022--2025 outdoor-trained
VPR zoo, frozen and without medical fine-tuning, against endoscopic
place-recognition ground truth. Off-the-shelf
models do appear as unmeasured components of endoscopic localization
pipelines~\cite{li2026automated,morlana2024colonmapper}, and training-free
foundation-model re-identification has been demonstrated in cadaver
endoscopy~\cite{soberanismukul2026global}.

\paragraph{Contributions.} Design and decision rules are fixed here, before
any data are acquired; every result slot is marked pending.
\begin{itemize}
\item[\textbf{C1}] A systematic zero-shot benchmark of the frozen
  2022--2025 VPR zoo (with NetVLAD as anchor), AnyLoc and raw DINOv2, under
  endoscopist and overlap-derived phantom ground truth, with a street-protocol
  transfer gap and a frozen endoscopy-pretrained arm.
\item[\textbf{C2}] The core contribution: a zoo-relative oracle ceiling
  (after~\cite{waheed2021improving}; the estimator of a companion
  street-scene study, reused unchanged), measured for the first time on
  endoscopic place-recognition ground truth, as far as we found. Its
  recoverable/irreducible split follows~\cite{vasconcelos2004minimum};
  ``irreducible'' always means \emph{for this zoo, this ground truth and this
  exclusion window}.
\item[\textbf{C3}] A feature-reliance diagnosis of place retrieval: cue
  suppression after~\cite{burgert2025imagenettrained} on the
  geometry-by-texture design of C3VDv2~\cite{golhar2026c3vdv2}, two-sided,
  with causal wording confined to phantoms.
\item[\textbf{C4}] A test of whether the ceiling structure found on streets
  recurs in anatomy and agrees with a TOPO-Bench-style ambiguity
  score~\cite{wang2025topobench}: evidence, not proof, of domain generality.
\end{itemize}
