\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Colonoscopy mapping produces disconnected submaps, and linking them or
detecting a revisit is a place-recognition
problem~\cite{morlana2024topological}. Retrieval networks fine-tuned on
colonoscopy now drive topological mapping and relocalization in real
procedures~\cite{morlana2024colonmapper,morlana2024topological}. Endoscopic
localization as image retrieval is thus established, with an
endoscopist-verified benchmark~\cite{ma2021colon10k} and full-procedure
data~\cite{azagra2023endomapper}. We claim no part of that formulation.

We ask a narrower question: when retrieval fails in the colon, \emph{whose}
failure is it? VPR-trained models degrade off-street, while DINOv2-based
ones, including training-free AnyLoc, hold up
best~\cite{keetha2024anyloc,larsen2026longterm}; the colon adds low texture,
specularities and deformation~\cite{schmidt2024tracking} and repetitive
tubular anatomy~\cite{jiang2025c3vdreg}. An error may be a failure of the
\emph{representation}, which another model could recover, or genuine
\emph{view ambiguity}: translation ambiguity along the tube already limits
registration on phantoms~\cite{jiang2025c3vdreg}, and mapping pipelines
discard frames humans judge unrecognizable~\cite{morlana2024colonmapper}.
The remedies differ, and no study we found separates the two for retrieval.

Zero-shot evidence exists: Morlana
et al.~\cite{morlana2021selfsupervised} ran a landmark- and city-trained
retrieval CNN off-the-shelf on a held-out real colonoscopy: it trailed its
colonoscopy-fine-tuned counterpart by only single-digit Recall@1 margins, and
its recall fell steeply as near-duplicate frames were excluded. Colon10k
itself evaluated existing place-recognition techniques~\cite{ma2021colon10k}
(which ones, and under what protocol, we could not determine). To our
knowledge (search to September 2026), no prior work systematically
benchmarks the 2022--2025 outdoor-trained VPR zoo, frozen and without medical
fine-tuning, against endoscopic place-recognition ground truth, although
off-the-shelf models already serve inside endoscopic pipelines
(Sec.~\ref{sec:related}).

\paragraph{Contributions.} Design and decision rules are specified here.
Every threshold is to be fixed, signed and registered in a public,
timestamped registry before any data are acquired; none is set yet
(Table~\ref{tab:prereg}), and every result slot is marked pending.
\begin{itemize}
\item[\textbf{C1}] A systematic zero-shot benchmark of the frozen
  2022--2025 VPR zoo (with NetVLAD as anchor), AnyLoc and raw DINOv2, under
  endoscopist and overlap-derived phantom ground truth, with a street-protocol
  transfer gap and a frozen endoscopy-pretrained arm.
\item[\textbf{C2}] The core contribution: a zoo ceiling, the any-correct
  oracle over a model zoo (after~\cite{waheed2021improving}), measured for the first
  time on endoscopic place-recognition ground truth, as far as we found, with
  a chance ceiling and an audit of the unsolved queries. It bounds per-query
  model selection only (Sec.~\ref{sec:ceiling}); ``zoo-unsolved'' always
  means \emph{for this zoo, this ground truth and this exclusion window}.
\item[\textbf{C3}] A feature-reliance diagnosis of place retrieval: cue
  suppression after~\cite{burgert2025imagenettrained} on the
  geometry-by-texture design of C3VDv2~\cite{golhar2026c3vdv2}, two-sided,
  with causal wording confined to phantoms.
\item[\textbf{C4}] The same ceiling statistics on the street data of the
  transfer gap, reported beside the colon values: a descriptive comparison,
  since domain and set-up are confounded~\cite{schubert2021makes}.
\end{itemize}
