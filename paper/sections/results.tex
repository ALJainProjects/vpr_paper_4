\paragraph{The gate.} The gate returned \texttt{\res{gate-outcome}}
(Table~\ref{tab:gate}). All \res{gate-nbeat} members beat chance by a wide
margin (smallest $\LB(\Drand)$: \res{pm-cosr101-clo}, CosPlace on
ResNet-101), and \res{gate-nnontriv} were non-trivial against the raw
thumbnail, which alone reached \res{bl-raw-r1} (\res{bl-raw-lo} to
\res{bl-raw-hi}; colour histogram \res{bl-hist-r1}). The best member,
\texttt{\res{gate-best}}, reached \res{pm-boqr50-r1} (\res{pm-boqr50-lo} to
\res{pm-boqr50-hi}) with $\LB(\Dhand)=\res{pm-boqr50-mlo}$; the smallest
passing bound is NetVLAD's, \res{pm-netvlad-mlo}. As the split condition was
disabled, \texttt{\res{gate-outcome}} rests on this count alone. The margins
over raw pixels are detectable but far smaller than those over chance.
Non-trivial
and trivial members occur in both the CNN and the DINOv2 families; the
planned contrast between families was not computed \pending{H-E1c: paired
case-bootstrap contrast of the CNN and DINOv2 means}. No query was
tie-sensitive for any member or handcrafted baseline (summed count
\res{ties-sum}), and the one empirical random ranking hit \res{bl-rand-hits}
queries, against \res{bl-rand-exp} expected by chance.

\paragraph{The zoo ceiling.} Some member retrieves a correct place for
$C=\res{or-ceil}$ of the queries (\res{or-ceil-lo} to \res{or-ceil-hi};
Table~\ref{tab:ceiling}), far above the chance ceiling of as many random
rankings, \res{or-chance}. For this zoo, this ground truth and this
exclusion window, $I=\res{or-irr}$ (\res{or-irr-lo} to \res{or-irr-hi}) of
the queries are zoo-unsolved. The best member misses $\rbest=\res{or-rec}$
(\res{or-rec-lo} to \res{or-rec-hi}) of the queries that another member
solves: \res{or-rec-share} of its errors are selection-recoverable, and
$\rbest$ is the headroom of per-query selection over it. We do not read $I$
as view ambiguity: the annotation is not known to be exhaustive, so a
zoo-unsolved query may be a correct retrieval of an unlabelled revisit, and
the planned blind audit of zoo-unsolved queries was not run
\pending{audit of zoo-unsolved queries for ground-truth misses}.

\paragraph{Per-case variation.} The test cases are few and unequal
(Table~\ref{tab:cases}): answerable queries per case range from
\res{pc-n-c08} (cases 08 and 13) to \res{pc-n-c19} (case 19), and the
window removed \res{pc-drop-c06} queries of case 06, leaving \res{pc-n-c06}.
The best member's per-case Recall@1 ranges from \res{pc-best-c08} (case 08)
to \res{pc-best-c13} (case 13), the two cases with \res{pc-n-c13} answerable
queries each. The case-level intervals rest on these clusters: the low power
stated in the protocol before the run.

\paragraph{Exclusion window (secondary).} As the window grows
(Table~\ref{tab:sweep}), answerable queries fall from \res{cnt-test5-ans} to
\res{cnt-test15-ans}, the thumbnail's recall falls, non-trivial members fall
from \res{cnt-test5-nontriv} to \res{cnt-test15-nontriv}, and the
zoo-unsolved fraction rises from \res{or-irr-w5} to \res{or-irr-w15},
consistent with near-duplicate frames inflating recall and the ceiling
alike~\cite{morlana2021selfsupervised}; whether a member is non-trivial
depends on the window. The headline stays at the pre-registered window.

\paragraph{Street comparison (cross-protocol).} Table~\ref{tab:street} sets
each member's colonoscopy Recall@1 against its street Recall@1 with the same
weights (checked by checksum), measured with the same harness on SF-XL
(small) and Nordland under a 25\,m threshold (Nordland: its frame
equivalent)~\cite{berton2022deep}. The two sides differ in ground-truth
kind, database, exclusion and frame rate, so $\Dm$ confounds domain with
set-up~\cite{schubert2021makes}. NetVLAD illustrates this: its colonoscopy
Recall@1 (\res{st-netvlad-colon}) exceeds its street Recall@1 on both SF-XL
(\res{st-sfxl-netvlad-street}) and Nordland (\res{st-nord-netvlad-street}).
The pre-registered two-sided test of $\Dm$ needs an interval, whose method
was chosen after the gate ran and is not reported \pending{H-E1a: Delta\_m
intervals, once the owner approves an interval method}. AnyLoc has no $\Dm$:
the street study measured AnyLoc-VLAD, the gate AnyLoc-GeM.
