\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

With thresholds, zoo, comparator and case split fixed before any model was
run in an internal pre-registration, frozen VPR models, most of them
street-trained, found the same place in real colonoscopy far above chance,
\res{gate-nnontriv} of \res{gate-nzoo} detectably better than raw pixels
(\texttt{\res{gate-outcome}}). An oracle per-query selector would recover
\res{or-rec-share} of the best member's errors, and \res{or-irr} of the queries remain zoo-unsolved for this zoo,
ground truth and window. For camera-carrying in-body robots, frozen
appearance-based relocalization is thus a candidate complement to external
sensing worth testing on robot video; we show no robot result and make no
clinical claim.
