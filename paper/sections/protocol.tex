\section{Pre-Specified Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

canonical for the gate, ground truths and baselines; this section restates
it and adds the E2 and E4 rules, to be merged into it. It is to be signed and
publicly registered with timestamps in two stages: Table~\ref{tab:prereg}
before any data are acquired, facts only the files give
(Sec.~\ref{sec:data}) before any descriptor is computed.

\paragraph{E1: zero-shot retrieval and the gate (Colon10k).} Every member
of $\Zall$ is run; we report Recall@$k$, $k\in\{1,5,10\}$, at $W^\star$ and
over $\mathcal{W}$, beside both baselines. Two-sided hypotheses:
\emph{H1a}, per member: the transfer gap $\Delta_m=R_m^{\mathrm{street}}-R_m$
(street side under the standard 25\,m protocol~\cite{berton2022deep})
differs from zero; the ground truths differ in kind~\cite{schubert2021makes},
so $\Delta_m$ does not isolate the domain. \emph{H1b}: each DINOv2-based VPR
model (SALAD, BoQ-DINOv2, MegaLoc, AnyLoc) differs from backbone-matched raw
DINOv2, CLS and GeM (VPR training or aggregation versus backbone). \emph{H1c}: the mean of
NetVLAD, CosPlace, EigenPlaces and MixVPR differs from that of the
DINOv2-based VPR models. $\Zout$ versus $\Zmed$ is descriptive. An interval
containing zero means ``no difference detected'', never equivalence.

\emph{Gate} (gate's $k$, $W^\star$, $\Zgate$). A member \emph{beats random}
if $\LB(R_m-\Rrand)>\delta_{\mathrm{rand}}$, and is \emph{non-trivial} if
also $\LB(R_m-\Rhand)>\delta_{\mathrm{hand}}$. The split is
\emph{meaningful} if $\LB(I)>I^\star$ and $\LB(\rho_{m^\star})>\rho^\star$,
with $m^\star$ the member of highest Recall@$k$, chosen once on the full
data; a bound of $-\infty$ disables its half (else no zoo that solves every
query reaches \textsc{Go}). \textsc{Go}: at least $n_{\mathrm{pass}}$ non-trivial members and a
meaningful split; E2 and E4 run. \textsc{No-go}: no member beats random; E1
is reported as a negative benchmark (no signal detected above the chance
margin),
and the study narrows to an anatomical-section probe or is shelved. Otherwise
\textsc{Owner review}: the protocol defines no action, and whatever follows
is reported as post hoc. $C$ and $\Crand(|\Zgate|)$ are reported
beside the split, not as gate terms. If ColonMapper's EndoMapper protocol
labels are obtainable, the zoo also runs beside its fine-tuned
descriptors~\cite{morlana2024colonmapper}.

\paragraph{E2: the ceiling in the body (Colon10k, C3VD, C3VDv2).} At the
gate's $k$, per ground truth, window in $\mathcal{W}$ and zoo (each arm, each backbone family,
$\Zall$): $C$, $I$, every $\rho_m$, headroom, $\Crand$, the zoo-size curve and
leave-one-out contributions. $I$ is \emph{large} if $\LB(I)>I^\star$ in two
or more backbone families, under the endoscopist and a geometric ground truth
(a conjunction: the two kinds also differ as real versus phantom data).
Adding members never increases $I$, so when $Z'=\Zgen$ or $\Zmed$ joins
$\Zout$, part of $\Zout$'s unsolved error is read as representation failure
only if $\LB(\Delta I-\Delta I_{\mathrm{rand}})>\delta_{\Delta}$, with
$\Delta I=I(\Zout)-I(\Zout\cup Z')$ and $\Delta
I_{\mathrm{rand}}=\Delta I_{\mathrm{rand}}(\Zout,|Z'|)$. $I(\Zall)$ is
\emph{stable} if $\UB$ of its spread (maximum minus minimum over the windows
in $\mathcal{W}$ and, on phantoms, the two geometric ground truths) is at most
$\varepsilon$; zoo size is not a stability axis. The ceiling is
\emph{saturated} if $\UB\bigl(\bar C(|Z|)-\bar C(|Z|-1)-g\bigr)<\eta$, with
$g$ the mean of $\Delta I_{\mathrm{rand}}(S,1)$ over subsets $S$ of size
$|Z|-1$ (the gain of one random ranking).

\emph{Audit.} Before any Colon10k query is read as view ambiguity, $n_a$
random zoo-unsolved queries are shown with every member's top-1 to annotators
blind to model identity, and labelled ground-truth miss (a retrieved frame
shows the query's place outside its intervals), ambiguous view (several
places fit), low quality, or representation failure (the place is
identifiable, yet no member retrieves it). Ambiguity is read only from the ``ambiguous''
share, and not at all if $\UB$ of the miss share exceeds $\gamma$. Unsolved
queries are also stratified by $\mathcal{B}_q$ (blur, specular area,
exposure). A large, stable $I$ that survives the audit would be evidence
that, for this zoo, colonoscopic place recognition is limited by view
ambiguity, in line with~\cite{jiang2025c3vdreg}; a small $I$ is a reportable
null (errors are mostly selection-recoverable); a ceiling near its chance
ceiling mostly collects lucky hits. For C4, the same statistics are reported
for the street data of H1a, with no recurrence verdict: domain and set-up
(ground truth, frame rate, window) are confounded~\cite{schubert2021makes}.

\paragraph{E4: aliasing geometry and feature reliance (phantoms).} (a)~For
each zoo-unsolved query, the displacement to the camera of the wrong top-1
of the dataset's best member is split into a component along the phantom
centreline, taken from the released mesh (else (a) is not run), and a
remainder. \emph{H4a}: the mean axial share differs from that of a
distance-matched null (one non-positive frame per query from the retrieved
frame's distance bin); registration results predict an axial
excess~\cite{jiang2025c3vdreg}, and no excess would not by itself identify a
cause. (b)~On C3VDv2, \emph{H4b}: the recall change under each manipulation
differs between the CNN- and DINOv2-based families (means). Manipulations:
cross- versus within-texture retrieval at fixed geometry, only if the
textures' shared frame is confirmed; debris-laden queries against their
clean twins (same places) plus debris-laden frames of other segments (same
condition, other places), after~\cite{kim2026visual}; cue suppression with
the operators of~\cite{burgert2025imagenettrained}, and grayscale. Causal
wording is confined to these contrasts, as ``in phantoms''.
