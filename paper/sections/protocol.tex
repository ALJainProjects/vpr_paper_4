\label{sec:protocol}

\paragraph{E1: zero-shot retrieval and the gate (Colon10k).} Every model in
$\Zall$ is run; we report Recall@$k$, $k\in\{1,5,10\}$, at $W^\star$ and over
$\mathcal{W}$. No large drop is hypothesized: the zero-shot gap
of~\cite{morlana2021selfsupervised} was small, and frozen DINOv2 transfers
well~\cite{dermyer2025endodino,parolari2026contrastive}.
\emph{H1 (two-sided)} predicts no direction for three paired Recall@1
contrasts: (a)~DINOv2-based minus CNN-based members of $\Zout$ (family
means); (b)~each DINOv2-based VPR model minus raw DINOv2 (VPR training versus
backbone); (c)~$\Zout$ minus $\Zmed$ (family means). An interval containing zero is reported as ``no
difference detected'', never as equivalence. The transfer gap (street
Recall@1 under the standard 25\,m protocol~\cite{berton2022deep}, run by our
harness, minus Colon10k Recall@1) is descriptive only, since the ground truths
differ in kind~\cite{schubert2021makes}. \emph{Gate.} With $\LB(\cdot)$ the
lower confidence bound of a paired sequence-bootstrap difference, G1
(signal) holds if some $m\in\Zall$ has $\LB(R_m-\Rrand)>\delta_{\mathrm{rand}}$
and $\LB(R_m-\Rhand)>\delta_{\mathrm{hand}}$; G2 (ceiling beyond chance) holds
if $\LB\bigl(C(\Zall)-\Crand(|\Zall|)\bigr)>\delta_{\mathrm{ceil}}$.
\textsc{Go} (G1 and G2): run E2--E5. \textsc{Partial} (G1 only): report E1,
report the Colon10k ceiling as a null, run E2 and E4 on phantoms only, and
drop C4. \textsc{No-go} (not G1): there is no signal to diagnose; E2, E4 and
E5 are not run on Colon10k, and the study narrows to E3 or is shelved:
\pending{pre-registered decision: E3-only or shelve on NO-GO}. If the labels
of ColonMapper's same-patient EndoMapper protocol (mAP) are obtainable, the
zoo also runs on it beside the published fine-tuned
descriptors~\cite{morlana2024colonmapper}.

\paragraph{E2: the ceiling in the body (Colon10k, C3VD, C3VDv2).} For each
ground truth (endoscopist, overlap, pose and angle), window in $\mathcal{W}$
and zoo ($\Zout$, $\Zgen$, $\Zmed$, $\Zall$) we report $C$, $I$, every
$\rho_m$, $\Crand$, the zoo-size curve and leave-one-out contributions. $I$
is called \emph{large} only if $\LB(I)>I^\star$ under both ground-truth
kinds (endoscopist and geometric) and in two or more feature families;
\emph{stable} only if its spread across ground truths, zoos and windows is
within $\varepsilon$; the ceiling is \emph{saturated} if the last member adds
less than $\eta$. Stratifying irreducible queries by $\mathcal{B}_q$ keeps
blurred or wall-contact frames from counting as aliasing. For C4,
the street structure \emph{recurs} if, as on streets, G2 holds, $I$ is stable
and the curve saturates; we add a TOPO-Bench-style
score~\cite{wang2025topobench} and state that domain and set-up are
confounded~\cite{schubert2021makes}.

\paragraph{E3: sections from frozen features (REAL-Colon).} Frozen
descriptors feed a $k$-nearest-neighbour classifier and a linear probe under
the multi-centre cross-validation of~\cite{biffi2025temporal}, holding out
patients and, separately, centres; we report macro-F1 and balanced accuracy.
A probe \emph{separates sections} if its macro-F1 exceeds chance by
$\delta_{\mathrm{probe}}$ at the lower confidence bound. The supervised
ColonTCN~\cite{biffi2025temporal} and EndoMapper region
baseline~\cite{azagra2023endomapper} are shown beside it as references, not
competitors.

\paragraph{E4: aliasing geometry and feature reliance (phantoms).} (a)~The
displacement between each query and its wrong top-1 retrieval is split into
a component along the local centreline (tangent of the smoothed trajectory)
and a cross-sectional remainder. H4a (two-sided): the axial share for
irreducible queries differs from that of random non-positive frames
(permutation test at level $\alpha$); registration results predict an axial
excess~\cite{jiang2025c3vdreg}. (b)~On C3VDv2 we contrast within- and
cross-texture retrieval at fixed geometry, clean and debris-laden twins as
distractors~\cite{kim2026visual}, and cue suppression
after~\cite{burgert2025imagenettrained}, including
grayscale~\cite{ismagilov2026channel}. H4b (two-sided): the recall change
under each manipulation differs between CNN and DINOv2 families. Causal
wording is confined to these contrasts, as ``in phantoms''; breakdowns on
real data are correlational. (c)~Colon10k's confident wrong matches are shown
by quality stratum.

\paragraph{E5 (optional; Go only): sequence priors.} The companion study's
temporal-prior retrieval is reported under full search, prior, prior-matched
control and random window, separating the signal the prior adds from
pruning; it is fit on $\mathcal{V}$.
