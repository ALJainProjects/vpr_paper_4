\section{Expected Outcomes and Their Interpretation}
\label{sec:outcomes}

\paragraph{E1.} If no model passes G1, the zoo carries no usable place
signal here: a reportable negative benchmark that rules out a Colon10k
ceiling. If only DINOv2-based members pass, that matches known frozen-DINOv2
transfer~\cite{dermyer2025endodino,huix2024natural}, not a new ``domain
gap'', and H1(b) says whether VPR training helps or hurts on top of the
backbone. A small gap across the zoo agrees
with~\cite{morlana2021selfsupervised} and makes the ceiling the main
question. A medical arm that beats the outdoor zoo, or fails to, is equally
reportable. Recall that rises at short windows reflects near-duplicates.

\paragraph{E2.} A large, stable irreducible fraction under both ground-truth
kinds and two or more feature families would be evidence that, for this
zoo, colonoscopic place recognition is limited by view ambiguity, in line
with~\cite{jiang2025c3vdreg}; it would remain a zoo-relative bound, not a
Bayes limit. If $I$ shrinks when $\Zgen$ or $\Zmed$ joins, part of the
outdoor zoo's ``irreducible'' error was representation failure. If $I$ moves
by more than $\varepsilon$ across ground truths or windows, that sensitivity
is the finding and C4 is withdrawn. A small $I$ is a reportable null: errors
are mostly recoverable, which points to model selection or fusion, not a
recognizability limit; a ceiling near its chance ceiling mostly collects
lucky hits.

\paragraph{E3--E4 and C4.} A frozen probe near the supervised references
would show that section identity is linearly available in frozen features;
one near chance, or collapsing across centres, would show that it is not,
or not portably. An axial excess
among irreducible queries would tie failure to tube geometry; its absence
points to texture, specularity or debris, which C3VDv2 separates in
phantoms. Either sign of H4b is informative, since the texture-bias premise
is contested~\cite{burgert2025imagenettrained}. Recurrence of the street
structure is evidence, not proof, that it is not street-specific;
non-recurrence is evidence that it is, or that set-up
dominates~\cite{schubert2021makes}. Agreement with the TOPO-Bench-style
score~\cite{wang2025topobench} would mean the oracle adds little to an
existing ambiguity measure.
